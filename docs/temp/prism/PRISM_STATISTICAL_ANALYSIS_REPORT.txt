SECTION 8: KEY ARCHITECTURAL & TRAINING PARAMETERS
================================================================================

MOTION VAE:
  • Temporal Convolution: Causal (for streaming generation)
  • Residual Blocks: 3 with causal temporal convolutions
  • Downsampling: 4x
  • Per-Frame Factorization: K=23 tokens per frame
    - 1 Root Token: 3D position + 3D velocity (6D)
    - 1 Global Orientation Token (6D - 6D rotation representation)
    - 21 Joint Rotation Tokens (6D rotation per joint)
  • Latent Dimension: D=16 per token (368 dims total per frame)
  • Training: 400K steps, 16 V100 GPUs, batch 64/GPU, float32
  • Max Sequence Length: 360 frames (~12s at 30fps)

FLOW-MATCHING DIFUSION TRANSFORMER (DiT):
  • Architecture: DiT-based (following Wan and ViMoGen)
  • Model Size: ~1.4B parameters
  • Blocks: 30
  • Text Conditioning: T5-XXL
  • Position Encoding: 2D RoPE along time and joint axes
  • Training: 300K steps, 80 V100 GPUs, batch 6/GPU, bfloat16
  • First-Frame Conditioning Probability: 0.5
  • Inference: 50 Euler steps with classifier-free guidance scale 5.0
  • Inference Speed: ~6s per 360-frame segment (~2x real-time on single V100)

TRAINING DATA:
  • Curated Dataset: ~200K high-quality SMPL(-X) motion-text pairs
  • Source: MotionHub (aggregates diverse open-source mocap datasets)
  • Representation: SMPL-22 pose, 30fps, standardized annotations

SECTION 9: SUMMARY OF KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. LATENT-GENERATOR ALIGNMENT PRINCIPLE:
   ✓ Joint-factorized 2D latent space (time × joints grid) fundamentally simplifies
     the generation problem
   ✓ Transforms one heterogeneous multi-scale regression (root trajectory in meters +
     per-joint rotations in radians) into K=23 homogeneous predictions
   ✓ Achieves 2.5x FID improvement with NO generator modification (controlled ablation)

2. TEXT-TO-MOTION STATE-OF-THE-ART:
   ✓ HumanML3D: FID 0.027 (55% improvement), R-Precision T1 0.699 (within 1.4% of real)
   ✓ MotionHub: FID 0.055 (48% improvement), R-Precision T1 0.530 (79.5% of real)

3. POSE-CONDITIONED GENERATION:
   ✓ Per-token timestep conditioning enables zero-architectural-modification conditioning
   ✓ Consistent 50%+ FID improvements across 1, 5, 9-frame conditioning scenarios

4. LONG-HORIZON SEQUENTIAL GENERATION:
   ✓ BABEL R@3: 0.587 (92% of real), Area Under Jerk: 0.440 (29% better than FlowMDM)
   ✓ Self-Forcing training suppresses drift in long rollouts

5. UNIFIED SINGLE-MODEL CAPABILITY:
   ✓ Single 1.4B model seamlessly handles: T2M, pose-conditioned, autoregressive
     sequential generation, and narrative motion composition
   ✓ User study: 76.4% preference vs MotionStreamer, 78.1% on transition smoothness

6. MOTION TOKENIZER QUALITY:
   ✓ 20x rFID improvement (0.001 vs 0.020), 18.6x MPJPE improvement (1.82mm vs 33.82mm)
   ✓ Faithful motion reconstruction enables high generation quality

7. RECONSTRUCTION-GENERATION GAP EXPLANATION:
   ✓ 20x rFID gain translates to ~2x FID generation gain due to:
     (a) Reconstruction headroom saturation (noise floor ~10^-3)
     (b) Generator becomes bottleneck with near-lossless tokenizer
     (c) Cross-paper deltas compress under common evaluator
   ✓ Latent design responsible for closing ~50% of gap to real motion

8. PHYSICS QUALITY TRADE-OFF:
   ✓ HY-Motion achieves better individual physics metrics (foot contact, ground penetration)
   ✓ PRISM optimizes for text-following fidelity and transition quality under unified constraints
