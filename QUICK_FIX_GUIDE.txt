================================================================================
                     SCIENCE BEHIND THE FIX
================================================================================

Second-order system response:

  Settling time formula: t_s = 4 / (ζ × ωₙ)
  
  ζ = 2.0 (current):  t_s = 4 / (2.0 × 62.83) ≈ 32ms error decay
                      Full settling: ~200ms
                      
  ζ = 1.0 (critical): t_s = 4 / (1.0 × 62.83) ≈ 64ms error decay
                      Full settling: ~100ms

At 50Hz control (20ms cycles):
  • ζ = 2.0: Takes 10 cycles just to settle to target
  • ζ = 1.0: Takes 5 cycles → time to respond to next disturbance

Gravity + terrain provide new disturbances every cycle.
With 10 cycles to settle but disturbances every cycle → FALLS.
With 5 cycles to settle and time between disturbances → STABLE.
