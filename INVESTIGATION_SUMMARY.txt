ROOT CAUSE
================================================================================

The SMPL humanoid falls in MuJoCo RL tracker simulation because:

1. MuJoCo backend does NOT implement self-collision disabling
2. All robot geoms allow mutual collision (conaffinity=1)
3. Standing pose has natural limb interpenetration
4. MuJoCo contact solver generates repulsive forces
5. These forces dominate PD control torques
6. Humanoid becomes unstable and falls

This is the ONLY major difference between MuJoCo and other simulators that
support the self_collisions flag.
