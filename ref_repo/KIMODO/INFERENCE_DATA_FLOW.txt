         │
         ▼
  INVERSE:        Extract global_root_heading from dims [3:5]
                  ├─ Convert [cos(ψ), sin(ψ)] → radians if needed
                  └─ Add to output_tensor_dict
         │
         ▼
  OUTPUT:         'global_root_heading' key in motion_dict
