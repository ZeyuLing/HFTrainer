9. KAFS MODES EXPLAINED
================================================================================

none:           Standard baseline (no per-joint timestep scaling)
depth_driven:   Per-joint scaling based on kinematic tree depth [RECOMMENDED]
uniform:        All joints get alpha=1.0 (sanity check)
random:         Random alphas in [0.85, 1.15] (ablation control)
