KNOWN ISSUES & RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

⚠ ISSUE 1: Contact Dimension Mismatch
  • Problem: Humanoid geoms use condim=1 (no friction)
  • Impact: Feet may slide on ground despite friction params
  • Fix: Change geom default to condim="3"

⚠ ISSUE 2: Shoulder Range Excessive
  • Problem: Shoulder joints allow ±720° (2 full rotations)
  • Impact: Unrealistic motion possible
  • Workaround: Add angle constraints in controller

⚠ ISSUE 3: No Explicit Foot Contact
  • Problem: Feet rely purely on collision detection
  • Workaround: ProtoMotions handles via non_termination_contact_bodies

✓ USE CASE RECOMMENDATIONS:

  RL Training:            Use smpl_humanoid.xml + ProtoMotions

  Motion Capture:         Use smpl_humanoid.xml (realistic mass)
