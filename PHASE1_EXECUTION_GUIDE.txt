================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING DURING TRAINING
================================================================================

Problem: Training starts but immediately crashes with CUDA error
Solution:
  1. Check GPU memory: nvidia-smi
  2. Verify CUDA paths: echo $CUDA_HOME
  3. Reduce num_envs from 16 to 8 (less GPU memory)
  4. Close other GPU processes

Problem: Training very slow (< 1k steps/hour)
Solution:
  1. Check GPU utilization: watch -n 1 nvidia-smi (should be >80%)
  2. Check CPU: top (should have 4+ cores at 100%)
  3. Increase num_envs to 32 (if GPU has room)
  4. Check disk I/O: iostat 1 10 (look for high wait%)

Problem: PPR not improving after 50k steps
Solution:
  1. Check reward structure: grep "reward_weights" training.log
  2. Increase physics weight to 0.6
  3. Check if policy is exploring: look at entropy in tensorboard
  4. Verify reference motions are correct: check first 100 steps PPR

Problem: FID increases beyond 0.75
Solution:
  1. Reduce physics reward weight to 0.4
  2. Increase text weight to 0.15
  3. Reduce learning rate by 2×
  4. Verify baseline FID is actually 0.537 (check metrics.json)

Problem: Loss explodes to NaN at step 12345
Solution:
  1. Reduce learning rates by 2-4×
  2. Reduce entropy coefficient
  3. Set gradient clipping to 0.5
  4. Resume from last checkpoint: --resume-checkpoint results/.../checkpoint_120000.pt

Debugging Utilities:
